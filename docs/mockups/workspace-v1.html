The Sunken Archive Investigation

Public Session Recap

The party descended into the flooded submerged vaults beneath the eastern cliffs. After dispatching the drowned guardians, they recovered two lore fragments detailing the ancient coastal pact. They discovered a locked iron safe containing Captain Elian Voss's private debt ledger.

Voss's daughter holds 500gp in debt markers with the Salt Knives. If the party confronts Voss with the ledger found here, he will break down and offer his harbor seals in exchange for silence.
